About Akshara Coaching Classes & Our Holistic Mission

Operated under Hiremeth Education Trust (R.) in Dharwad, Akshara Coaching Classes empowers Standards 4 to 10 students across Karnataka State Board, CBSE, competitive residential school entrance pathways, and enriching summer programmes — with full leadership & educator profiles dedicated on our Team page.

INSTITUTIONAL VISION

Inspiring Confident Young Learners Across North Karnataka

To cultivate intellectually fearless, conceptually clear, and culturally expressive young learners across Dharwad and North Karnataka — ready for premier residential schools, board excellence, and lifelong learning.

App: Akshara classes dharwad (Classplus App)·YouTube: @rakeshhiremath5391
CORE MISSION PILLARS

How We Teach & Nurture Students

  • Eliminate rote memorisation through bilingual English & Kannada conceptual clarity across Mathematics, Science, Languages & Mental Ability.
  • Provide structured 6-stage revision cycles and realistic OMR / board-blueprint testing without overwhelming young learners.
  • Celebrate holistic childhood via annual Sneha Sammelana cultural stages, traditional Lagori & outdoor athletics, indoor Carrom, and faculty-supervised excursions.
  • Uphold complete transparency with parents regarding official eligibility rules, verified campus directions, residential inspection, and ethical outcomes.
