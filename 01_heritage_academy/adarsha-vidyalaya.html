SECTION 02 · ELIGIBILITY & PATHWAY

Who Can Enrol?

  • Open to students studying in Class 5 within the respective taluk/jurisdiction as notified by the Department of School Education & Literacy (Karnataka) / KSEAB.
  • Age, domicile, and school study certificates strictly follow the annual Adarsha Vidyalaya Class 6 entrance notification.

Source: Dept. of School Education Karnataka / KSEAB Adarsha Notification

SECTION 05 & 07 · METHODOLOGY & ASSESSMENT

Teaching & Testing System

Classroom Method: Synchronised mastery of both Kannada-medium textbook concepts and English terminology so rural and semi-urban students transition smoothly into English-medium Adarsha Vidyalayas.

Assessments: Weekly 100-mark 5-subject OMR tests reflecting official taluk-level difficulty and bubble-marking practice.

SECTION 03 · INTERACTIVE SUBJECT-WISE SYLLABUS

Complete Curriculum Breakdown

Kannada & English Grammar (40 Marks)

  • Kannada Vyakarana: Sandhi, Samasa, Vibhakti, Tatsama-Tadbhava & Reading Passages
  • English Grammar: Tenses, Articles, Prepositions, Antonyms/Synonyms & Sentence Order

Mathematics (20 Marks)

  • Place value, 4 fundamental operations, Fractions, Decimals, Money & Time
  • LCM/HCF, Perimeter, Area & Word Problems from Class 4–5 Karnataka DSERT textbooks

Science & Social Science (40 Marks)

  • Food & Nutrition, Human Organ Systems, Plants, Light, Air & Water
  • Karnataka Districts, Rivers, Freedom Struggle, Constitution & Civics

SECTION 06 · MANDATORY 6-STAGE REVISION SYSTEM

Visual Revision Roadmap (No Fabricated Timetables)

Every Akshara batch progresses through six structured revision cycles leading up to examination day. Exact batch test dates are confirmed directly at the centre.

Stage 01

Topic-Wise Concept Revision

Structured chapter recap right after syllabus completion so foundational formulas and definitions remain crisp.

Stage 02

Subject Consolidation Blocks

Connecting interrelated chapters across Mathematics, Reasoning, Science, and Languages through summary sheets.

Stage 03

High-Yield Important Concepts

Focused review of high-weightage exam topics (e.g., AISSEE 50% Maths weightage or JNVST spatial figures).

Stage 04

Practice Banks & Timed Model Papers

Daily objective MCQ practice sets and official-format OMR / board-blueprint model papers under clock pressure.

Stage 05

Error-Log Review & Doubt Remediation

Individual error correction workshops addressing calculation slips, misread stems, and OMR bubbling accuracy.

Stage 06

Final Pre-Exam Readiness Sprints

Full-length mock examinations and calm exam-hall pacing (Exact test calendar is confirmed batch-wise at campus).
