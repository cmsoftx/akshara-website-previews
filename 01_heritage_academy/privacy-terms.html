Privacy Policy, Minor Consent & Institutional Terms

Akshara Coaching Classes under Hiremeth Education Trust (R.) protects student privacy, parental consent rights, and factual transparency across all digital pages.

01 · CHILD PRIVACY & MEDIA CONSENT

Minors Photography & Video Governance

Child Privacy & Consent Governance: Authentic photographs and videos featuring identifiable students under 18 are published subject to written parent/guardian consent held by Hiremeth Education Trust.

Parents or guardians who wish any authentic photograph or classroom video clip removed or updated may email aksharanavodayasainikschool@gmail.com or call +91 95358 18800 for immediate redressal.

02 · ETHICAL DATA & ENQUIRY HANDLING

Zero Misleading Claims & Form Privacy

  • These static HTML websites never sell parent mobile numbers or run third-party advertising trackers.
  • The admission enquiry form validates inputs locally and hands off pre-filled queries solely when the parent taps the official WhatsApp desk button.
