Pathways to Leading Schools & Institutions

Explore the educational pathways supported by Akshara's entrance preparation programmes.

JNVST · Class 6 & 9

Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (JNVST)

Central residential co-educational CBSE schools selection test covering Mental Ability, Arithmetic, and Kannada/English comprehension.

RMS CET · Ministry of Defence

Rashtriya Military Schools (RMS CET)

Five Ministry of Defence residential campuses including Belagavi & Bengaluru — qualifying English, Intelligence, Maths, GK & Interview readiness.

Explore RMS CET Course →
NTA AISSEE · Boys & Girls

Sainik Schools Society (AISSEE)

All India Sainik Schools Entrance Exam preparation highlighting the 50% Mathematics weightage, Intelligence, GK & Language papers.

Explore Sainik Course →
Alike · Residential Entry

Sri Sathya Sai Loka Seva Gurukulam (Alike)

Values-anchored written preparation in Mathematics & Bilingual Languages alongside calm student-parent personal interaction coaching.

Explore Alike Prep →
Alva's · Merit & Scholarship

Alva's Education Foundation (Moodabidri)

Analytical Mathematics, Conceptual Science, English proficiency & co-curricular interview readiness for Moodabidri admissions.

Explore Alva's Prep →

Army Public School (APS)

CBSE/NCERT Maths, Science & English foundational sharpening tailored for families preparing children for Army Public School admissions (dedicated APS batch status confirmed at campus).
