Sainik School Coaching (NTA AISSEE)

Focused preparation for boys and girls appearing in the National Testing Agency (NTA) All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE) for Class 6 and Class 9 residential entry.

SECTION 02 · ELIGIBILITY & PATHWAY

Who Can Enrol?

  • Class 6 Entry: Boys & girls aged 10 to 12 years as on 31 March of the admission year, studying in/passed Class 5.
  • Class 9 Entry: Boys & girls aged 13 to 15 years as on 31 March of the admission year, studying in/passed Class 8 from a recognised school.
  • Final seat allocation, reservation categories, and medical fitness strictly follow the official NTA AISSEE Bulletin (exams.nta.ac.in/AISSEE).

Source: National Testing Agency (exams.nta.ac.in/AISSEE)

SECTION 05 & 07 · METHODOLOGY & ASSESSMENT

Teaching & Testing System

Classroom Method: Priority mastery of the high-weightage Mathematics paper (150/300 marks in Class 6; 200/400 in Class 9) paired with structured weekly Defence GK bulletins and disciplined morning/evening routine.

Assessments: Full-length 300-mark (Class 6) and 400-mark (Class 9) simulated OMR mock tests checking qualifying threshold (25% per section, 40% aggregate) in every subject.

SECTION 03 · INTERACTIVE SUBJECT-WISE SYLLABUS

Complete Curriculum Breakdown

Mathematics (50% Total Weightage)

  • Class 6: Natural Numbers, LCM/HCF, Unitary Method, Fractions, Ratio & Proportion, Profit/Loss, Simplification
  • Class 6 Geometry: Lines & Angles, Triangles, Circles, Perimeter, Area, Volume of Cubes/Cuboids, Roman Numerals, Temperature
  • Class 9: Rational Numbers, Linear Equations, Algebraic Identities, Factorization, Exponents, Quadrilaterals, Mensuration, Probability & Data

Intelligence & Logical Reasoning

  • Verbal & Non-Verbal Analogies and Classification
  • Number & Alphabetical Series, Coding-Decoding
  • Direction Sense, Blood Relations, Mirror/Water Images & Paper Folding

General Knowledge / Class 9 Science & Social Studies

  • Indian Defence Forces, Ranks, Gallantry Awards & Monuments
  • Rivers, Mountains, National Parks, Science in Everyday Life & Sports Trophies
  • Class 9 NCERT Science (Force, Pressure, Sound, Metals, Cells) & History/Civics/Geography

Language Comprehension & Grammar

  • Parts of Speech, Tenses, Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect Narration
  • Idioms, Synonyms/Antonyms, Ordering of Words & Reading Passages

SECTION 06 · MANDATORY 6-STAGE REVISION SYSTEM

Visual Revision Roadmap (No Fabricated Timetables)

Every Akshara batch progresses through six structured revision cycles leading up to examination day. Exact batch test dates are confirmed directly at the centre.

Stage 01

Topic-Wise Concept Revision

Structured chapter recap right after syllabus completion so foundational formulas and definitions remain crisp.

Stage 02

Subject Consolidation Blocks

Connecting interrelated chapters across Mathematics, Reasoning, Science, and Languages through summary sheets.

Stage 03

High-Yield Important Concepts

Focused review of high-weightage exam topics (e.g., AISSEE 50% Maths weightage or JNVST spatial figures).

Stage 04

Practice Banks & Timed Model Papers

Daily objective MCQ practice sets and official-format OMR / board-blueprint model papers under clock pressure.

Stage 05

Error-Log Review & Doubt Remediation

Individual error correction workshops addressing calculation slips, misread stems, and OMR bubbling accuracy.

Stage 06

Final Pre-Exam Readiness Sprints

Full-length mock examinations and calm exam-hall pacing (Exact test calendar is confirmed batch-wise at campus).
